Key Insight for Judges: Ask each team: How many chunks did your vector store have? An unoptimised pipeline
will produce ~600+ chunks. A well-designed one will produce ~180–220 meaningful chunks. More importantly —
ask: 'Can your system answer Q:H1 (Dream11 for KKR vs RCB)?' If yes, they implemented multi-node routing
correctly. If it returns a generic answer, they are still using a single-chain approach.
