■ IPL Intelligence Assistant Dataset
 Multimodal RAG Exercise with LangGraph
 SVECW · Department of Information Technology · RAG-ATHON 24 — Advanced Track
Why IPL + LangGraph? The Placement dataset taught you a single-chain RAG pipeline using LangChain. This
dataset is designed to force you to think in graphs, not chains. IPL queries are naturally multi-agent: a Dream11
recommendation needs a Player Stats Agent, a Pitch Conditions Agent, and a Recent Form Agent to collaborate. A
match prediction needs conditional routing based on whether the query is about batting, bowling, or all-round
performance. LangGraph models this as a stateful directed graph — nodes are agents, edges are routing
decisions, and the state flows between them. This dataset gives you the corpus to build that system.
